ether.fi Dashboard

ether.fi is the largest liquid restaking protocol and a full onchain neobank — spanning eETH & eBTC restaking, the Liquid vault suite, the ETHFI token, and the Cash card program. This dashboard tracks restaked asset value, token economics, card activity and protocol financials across the entire ether.fi ecosystem.
